Set up a device
Pre-filled from the address you are reading this page on. If SENTRY sits behind a proxy or a CDN, flow records do not follow that path — put the collector's own reachable address here instead.
Register a device
The IP address the device's flow packets arrive from, which is not always the address you manage it on. A name will not work here: the collector matches the literal source address on every datagram.
The node label its flows are filed under, so they line up with the rest of the dashboard. Left blank, the device's name is used.
Leave at 1 unless the device is configured to sample. A device that reports one flow in every thousand describes a flood as a trickle, and the detector believes it.
Remove this device

Flow records already collected are kept. They are evidence of what happened on the network, and removing a device from the inventory is not a claim its traffic never occurred. What stops is future ingest: anything it sends from now on is discarded as unregistered.
