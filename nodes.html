Rename this node
Renaming carries the history with it: every flow and incident already recorded under the old name is updated to the new one. The device is the same device — only what we call it changed. Leaving those rows behind would strand the node's past traffic under a name nothing answers to, and the renamed node would read as newly installed.

Remove this node

Its flows and incidents are kept. They are evidence of what happened on the network, and taking a device off the inventory is not a claim its traffic never occurred. Note that a node which is still sending will register itself again within a tick or two — for a network monitor, something still talking still exists. To actually stop collecting from it, remove the device on the Exporters page.
